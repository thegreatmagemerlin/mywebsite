About

Keep learning.
Keep building.

I'm Barath, a biomedical engineering student at Monash University. I work on personal projects driven either by my own interests and needs, or by ideas I stumble across that are too interesting to ignore. Outside of engineering, I work as a private tutor. I've been fortunate enough to understand maths and science well, and I think that comes with a responsibility to pass it on.

I'm also a gymrat, and have been training since high school. I train for hypertrophy, but the mental benefits are just as real to me. Exercise puts me in a better mood and genuinely helps me think more clearly. It's one of those things that improves every other part of your life just by doing it consistently.

My passion for science started young. Star Wars, Iron Man, Spiderman, and later anime and manga, all of it pushed me toward STEM and shaped how I think about what engineering is for.

At the end of the day, whether I'm in the lab, the gym, or lost in a fictional world, I just want to keep learning, keep building, and help make life a little better, for myself and for others.
